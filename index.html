Jellyfish Climb is a free browser game about rhythm, direction, and choosing when to move. Collect plankton on the way up, work with changing water currents, and reach the goal while time remains.

Game overview

Simple input, careful timing

The game can be understood without loading its canvas: click or tap to make the jellyfish pulse upward. Aim with A and D on a keyboard, or tilt a supported mobile device. A well-timed pulse climbs more strongly than an early or late one.

Time the pulse

Watch the expanding pulse ring. Timing determines whether the move is judged EARLY, PERFECT, or LATE.

Read the current

Upward and downward flow phases change over time, so the same move can have a different result.

Build the score

Collect moving plankton, then reach the goal. The remaining time determines the Time Rate applied to the plankton score.
